# Moss & Mail — Instagram Growth Strategy
Last updated: 2026-05-05

---

## The Goal
Build an audience of people who have never heard of a letter subscription but are exactly the person who would love one. Grow fast enough to launch June 8 with momentum. Stay completely true to Kayli's voice and realness. No gimmicks.

---

## How the Algorithm Works Right Now (May 2026)

### The three signals that determine reach — in order of importance:
1. **Sends** — when someone DMs a post to a friend. This is the #1 signal. Instagram treats it as "this content is worth showing to strangers." Every caption should give people a reason to send it to someone.
2. **Watch time past 3 seconds** — for Reels. If 60%+ of viewers stay past 3 seconds, the algorithm pushes it. Below 40%, it stalls. The opening moment of every video determines everything.
3. **Saves** — especially for carousels and static posts. A save tells Instagram "I want to come back to this." Saves = evergreen reach.

Likes matter least. Comments matter for community but not reach.

### What Instagram rewards in 2026:
- Original, unfiltered footage over polished production
- Consistent posting (3–4x/week) over daily posting that can't be maintained
- Keyword-rich captions (Instagram now indexes captions for search, not just hashtags)
- Niche specificity — accounts that are clearly about something get pushed to the right people faster
- Trial Reels — a feature that shows your Reel to non-followers first to test reach potential before it hits your existing audience. Use this on every new Reel.

## Caption Formula (every post)

**First 125 characters:** must contain a searchable keyword
- slow living / slow morning / intentional motherhood / Charlotte Mason / snail mail / handwritten letter / analog living / nature study

**Body:** written like a paragraph from a letter. Never a list. Never bullet points. Kayli's voice — honest, specific, unhurried.

**Send prompt** (every caption): a reason to share
- "Send this to the mom who needs a slow morning."
- "If you know someone who still writes letters, they need to see this."
- NOT: "Tag a friend!" — that reads as a prompt. Make it feel like a genuine thought.

**Hashtags:** 3–5 only. Highly specific beats volume.
- Core: #slowliving #intentionalmotherhood #charlottemason #snailmail #mossandmail
- Rotate in: #analoglife #slowschool #naturalstudy #handwrittenletter #letterclub

**Alt text:** every single post. Formula: [what's in the video/image] — [keyword phrase] — Moss & Mail

## Engagement Strategy — Who to Engage With

### You are RIGHT. Following other letter clubs is wrong.

Following and engaging with competitor accounts (other letter subscriptions, mail clubs) does three things, all bad:
1. Fills your feed with competitors
2. Signals to Instagram that you're in a crowded niche
3. Gets you seen by people who already know this type of product exists

**The goal is to be found by people who don't know letter subscriptions exist yet and are thrilled when they find it.**

### Engage with your TARGET CUSTOMER'S community — not your competitors'.

Who buys Moss & Mail? A mom who loves slow living, books, nature, intentional choices, handmade things, Charlotte Mason philosophy, analog life.

**Where she already hangs out on Instagram:**

| Community | Example account types |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Charlotte Mason / homeschool | Curriculum accounts, nature study, living books |
| Slow living / simple life | Cottagecore, simple homemaking, analog aesthetics |
| Nature journaling / watercolor | Botanical illustration, nature walks, sketchbooks |
| Intentional motherhood | Present parenting, screen-free childhood |
| Book culture | Bookstagram, literary lifestyle, reading with children |
| Handmade / artisan | Wax seals, hand lettering, paper goods |

**Engagement rule:** Comment genuinely on posts in THOSE communities. Not "beautiful!" — something real and specific. Instagram's algorithm registers that interaction and starts showing your profile to people who also engage with those accounts. That's how you get discovered by the right person.

**Following rule:** Follow accounts in adjacent communities (above), not competitors. Follow sparingly — quality over quantity. Your feed should inspire Kayli and inform content, not fill up with mail clubs.

## Carousel Strategy

Carousels get the highest save rate of any format. Use them for:
- "5 books we read this month" (saves for the list)
- "What's inside a Moss & Mail envelope" (saves for the reveal)
- Nature photo series with a letter excerpt (saves for the beauty)
- Journaling prompt spread (saves for the prompt)

**Carousel structure:**
- Slide 1: the hook — strongest image + single line of text
- Slides 2–6: the content
- Last slide: soft CTA — "Join the waitlist" or "Letters open June 8"

## What NOT to Do

- No trending audio for the sake of trending audio — only if it genuinely fits
- No "follow for more" — says nothing real
- No "drop a 🌿 if you relate" — feels manufactured
- No posting daily if it means rushing content
- No following 500 accounts at once — looks like a bot, hurts reach
- No hashtag stuffing — 3–5 targeted beats 30 generic every time

---

## Sunday Analytics Review (Weekly)

Every Sunday: pull Publer analytics, log in Google Sheet:
- Which posts got the most saves this week
- Which posts got the most sends (DMs — Publer may not track this; check IG insights)
- Which Reels crossed 60% 3-second retention
- What's not performing — identify and don't repeat the format

## The Big Picture

Kayli's realness IS the strategy. The algorithm in 2026 rewards unfiltered, specific, niche content from real people. The job is not to turn her into an influencer. The job is to make sure the algorithm understands who she is and gets her in front of the right people. Every caption, every alt text, every hashtag choice is a signal to Instagram that says: "this is for slow living moms who love books and letters." The more consistent and specific that signal is, the faster the right people find it.
